== Installation ==

= Automatic Installation =

1. Log in to your WordPress admin panel
2. Navigate to Plugins → Add New
3. Search for "ConversaPay AI Chat"
4. Click "Install Now" and then "Activate"

= Manual Installation =

1. Download the plugin file `conversapay-chat.php`
2. Upload it to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory via FTP or hosting file manager
3. Go to Plugins in your WordPress admin
4. Find "ConversaPay AI Chat Assistant" and click "Activate"

= Configuration =

1. After activation, go to **Settings → ConversaPay Chat**
2. Enter your **Business ID** (found in your ConversaPay Dashboard)
3. Configure widget settings:
   - **Position**: Choose bottom-right or bottom-left
   - **Color**: Select your brand color
   - **Pages**: Choose where to display (all pages, homepage only, or selected pages)
4. Click "Save Settings"
5. The widget will now appear on your configured pages

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Where do I get my Business ID? =

Sign up for a free account at [conversapay.org](https://conversapay.org), create your business in the dashboard, and copy your Business ID from Settings → Integrations.

= Is there a free plan? =

Yes! We offer a free Sandbox plan with up to 5 test orders. The Pro plan ($29/month) includes unlimited orders, advanced features, and priority support.

= Does it work with WooCommerce? =

Yes! You can sync your WooCommerce products with ConversaPay or use our native product management system.

= Is the chatbot really AI-powered? =

Yes! We use Google's Gemini AI to power our chatbot, providing natural, context-aware conversations that understand your products and customers.

= Can I customize the widget appearance? =

Absolutely! You can customize the widget color, position, and even the AI's personality and greeting message from your dashboard.

= Is it mobile-friendly? =

Yes! The widget is fully responsive and works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

= How do payments work? =

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. When a customer decides to purchase, the chatbot creates a Stripe checkout session and provides a secure payment link.

= Can I see analytics and reports? =

Yes! Your ConversaPay Dashboard provides detailed analytics including conversations, conversion rates, revenue, and popular products.

= Is my data secure? =

Yes! We use bank-level encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all communications. We never store sensitive payment information on our servers. All payments are processed directly through Stripe.

= What if I need help? =

We offer comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and email support. Pro users get priority support with faster response times.
